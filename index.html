📖 Educational Walkthrough
① IDLE StateMachine waiting. All outputs OFF. Door locked = ready signal.
② SOAK → water fills drumInlet valve OPEN. Water level sensor rises. Timer counts.
③ WASH → drum agitatesMotor ON. Direction alternates every 2.5s. RPM = program-defined.
④ RINSE → drain + refillDrain pump ON. Water exits then refills. Medium rotation.
⑤ SPIN → high-speed centrifugeMax RPM. Strong vibration. Drum glows purple. WOW moment.
⑥ FAULT → exception handlingAny state can → FAULT. All outputs stop. Auto-recovery demo.
